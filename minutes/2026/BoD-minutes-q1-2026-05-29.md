## 3. Quarterly Financial Overview and Financial Action Plan

**Issue before the Board:** The Board reviewed the organisation’s financial position, the presentation of budget performance, projected cash runway and contingency planning.

**Information presented:** Management reported that available funds were projected to extend through the end of October and that new grant funding was not progressing quickly enough to extend that date. The Financial Action Plan prioritised gap funding, grant development and increased programme-related revenue.

Management also presented a minimum-staffing contingency if additional funding was not secured, with a decision anticipated by 15 August and implementation by the end of September. Intermediate options had been considered but generally did not provide sufficient additional runway. The Board noted that any minimum-staffing period would require continued Board and Finance Committee engagement and clearer criteria for duration and recovery.

**Board action:** The Board received the financial overview and Financial Action Plan. During the agenda item, the Board approved a procedural motion to extend the discussion by ten minutes. Stephanie seconded the motion; the mover was not recorded. No substantive financial resolution was adopted.

**Follow-up:**

* Danielle will schedule an additional Board discussion before the planned meeting with John concerning financial sustainability.
* Management will continue pursuing gap funding, new grants and programme-related revenue.
* The Board and Finance Committee will increase their engagement as funding and staffing scenarios develop.

## 5. Board Appointments and Composition

**Summary:** The Board considered temporary non-compliance in its composition, the vacant Secretary office and the Board liaison role to the Code of Conduct Committee.

**Information presented:** The Board reviewed options for addressing its composition before the October election, including an interim community appointment. The Board determined that stronger engagement from current directors was the immediate priority and noted that non-voting Board Advisors could be considered where additional expertise or capacity would be useful.

**Board action:**

* Phil moved to maintain the current Board composition until the October election, when compliance would be addressed through the regular election process. Eboni seconded. The motion passed with 8 votes in favour and 0 opposed.
* Yani moved to appoint Luke as Secretary of The Carpentries Board of Directors. François seconded. The motion passed with 7 votes in favour and 0 opposed.
* Yani moved to appoint François as the Board of Directors Liaison to the Code of Conduct Committee. Pao seconded. The motion passed with 7 votes in favour and 0 opposed.

**Follow-up:**

* Board composition will be brought into compliance through the October election process.
* Directors may propose potential Board Advisors who can contribute relevant expertise and active support.
* Luke will assume the duties of Secretary.
* François will serve as Board liaison to the Code of Conduct Committee.

The discussion also considered whether programme audiences and outcomes should broaden as AI reshapes the research cycle.
