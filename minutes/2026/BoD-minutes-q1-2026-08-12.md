## 3. Leadership Transition Communications

**Summary:** The Board reviewed communications concerning the Executive Team transition.

**Information presented:** The proposed messaging explained that the leadership transition was connected to the organisation’s financial position and formed part of an established planning process. The Board considered how to communicate the transition clearly without creating unnecessary alarm and how to recognise Kari’s accomplishments and completion of key objectives during her tenure.

The publication date for the announcement was moved from 18 August to 25 August 2026.

**Board action:** The Board reviewed the transition communications and provided direction on the content, tone and timing. No formal vote was recorded.

**Follow-up:**

* Phil will publish the blog post as part of the transition communications plan.
* The announcement will be published on 25 August 2026.
* Board members will review the communications from their respective governance and stakeholder perspectives and identify any concerns with the content or messaging.
* The communications will recognise Kari’s accomplishments and successful completion of key objectives.

## 4. Board and Committee Responsibilities

**Summary:** The Board considered how its working practices should change while Board administrative support is reduced to half time.

**Information presented:** Board members were advised that administrative reminders and scheduling support would be limited. Standing committee chairs would need to take greater responsibility for organising committee meetings, and all directors would need to manage their Board responsibilities proactively.

**Board action:** No formal vote was recorded. The Board acknowledged the need for increased engagement and self-management.

**Follow-up:**

* Standing committee chairs will schedule their committee meetings.
* Board members will manage their responsibilities and deadlines with limited administrative reminders.

## 5. Treasurer Role Description

**Summary:** The revised role description for the Treasurer of The Carpentries Board of Directors was presented for approval.

**Information presented:** The Board reviewed the responsibilities assigned to the Treasurer.

**Board action:** Yani moved to approve The Carpentries Board Treasurer role description. Pao seconded. The motion was approved by consensus.

**Follow-up:** The approved Treasurer role description will be adopted for Board use.

## 6. Board Composition and Skills Matrix

**Summary:** The Board considered the experience, skills and capacity needed in future directors.

**Information presented:** The Board identified the need for directors who have sufficient time to participate actively and experience serving nonprofit organisations. Priority areas included:

* Artificial intelligence
* New product development and revenue generation
* Innovation and organisational change
* Fund development, grant writing and fundraising
* Professional development
* Cross-sector partnerships
* Experience with revenue models beyond philanthropic funding
* Experience working across different geographic and funding contexts
* Alignment with The Carpentries’ values

The Board also considered its authority to appoint additional directors, subject to limits on Board size and requirements for community representation.

**Follow-up:** Yani will host two Board recruitment information sessions on 20 August 2026 covering Board service and the solicitation process.
